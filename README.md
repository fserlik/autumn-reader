# Autumn Reader

Autumn Reader is a calm, customizable place to read and organize the PDF and EPUB books you already own. It is available for Windows and Android, with an interface in English, Spanish, Italian, and French.

## Download

[Visit autumnreader.lat](https://autumnreader.lat/#descargas) to get the current version and see platform availability.

## Features

- Keep local and synchronized books together in one organized library.
- Read EPUB files and PDF documents in their original layout or an adaptable text view.
- Adjust typography, spacing, columns, margins, and book themes without changing the rest of the app.
- Save progress, favorites, colored notes, reviews, and private reading lists.
- Search inside compatible books and translate selected passages.
- Listen with system voices or locally imported Piper and Kokoro voices, including offline.
- Use the optional Autumn Pro cloud voice and manage its monthly allowance from Settings.
- Review a private reading journal with streaks, calendars, daily activity, and time by book.
- Export the statistics panel as a JPG to share it without uploading the image anywhere.
- Sign in with email or Google and choose which books to synchronize between devices.

Local books do not count toward cloud storage and there is no numeric book limit. Free includes 1 GiB of cloud storage and two active devices. Autumn Pro includes 25 GiB, unlimited devices, additional themes, cloud voice, and higher translation allowances.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Access |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | US$0 | Permanent |
| Autumn Pro monthly | US$5.99/month | Renews monthly |
| Autumn Pro Lifetime | US$119.99 once | One-time purchase |

Both Autumn Pro options unlock the same features and monthly cloud allowances. Lifetime means permanent access to Autumn Pro under the purchase terms; third-party cloud services remain subject to monthly and shared safety limits.

## Framework

Autumn Reader is built with TypeScript, Vite, Tauri, and Rust. The project is open source under the [MIT license](LICENSE).
