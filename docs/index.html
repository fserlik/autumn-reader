Built around your library

More than a reader. A home for the books you already own.

Import PDF and EPUB files, keep them organized, and return to the exact place where you stopped. Autumn Reader is designed to stay out of the way while you read.

01

PDF + EPUB

Your books, in one library

Add multiple PDF and EPUB books from your device. Covers, recent reads and favorites make it easy to find your way back.

02

Reading progress

Continue where you left off

Autumn Reader saves your position as you read, so reopening a book takes you back to your last page or section.

03

Notes

Keep ideas attached to the text

Select a passage, choose a marker color and write a note. Open your notes later and jump straight back to the passage.

04

Reader controls

Make the page comfortable

Adjust text size and book font when the format allows it, and switch between light and dark themes from the app settings.

05

4 languages

Use Autumn Reader in your language

The app interface is available in English, Spanish, Italian and French.
